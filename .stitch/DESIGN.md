# Shift Delivery App & ERP - Design System

**Project Name:** Shift Logistics
**Brand Tone:** Professional, Reliable, Fast, Modern

### 1. Color Palette
- **Primary:** Deep Blue `#1E3A8A` (Trust, Logistics)
- **Secondary:** Emerald Green `#10B981` (Success, Delivered, Start Shift)
- **Accent/Highlight:** Sky Blue `#3B82F6` (Active elements)
- **Background:** Light Gray `#F3F4F6` (App background)
- **Surface:** White `#FFFFFF` (Cards, panels)
- **Text (Primary):** Dark Slate `#111827`
- **Text (Secondary):** Cool Gray `#6B7280`

### 2. Typography
- **Primary Font:** Inter or Roboto (sans-serif)
- **Headings:** Bold, clean, well-spaced
- **Body:** Readable, neutral

### 3. Visual Language
- **Corner Radius:** `12px` to `15px` for cards and buttons (friendly, modern)
- **Shadows:** Soft drop shadows on cards to elevate them from the background.
- **Icons:** Material Icons.

### 4. Layout & Spacing
- **Padding:** Generous padding (20px - 24px) around containers.
- **Alignment:** Centered logins, full-width buttons.

### 6. Design System Notes for Stitch Generation
**DESIGN SYSTEM (REQUIRED):**
Use a professional logistics aesthetic with a `#1E3A8A` primary blue and `#10B981` success green. Backgrounds should be a clean `#F3F4F6` with crisp `#FFFFFF` cards. Use generous `15px` rounded corners and soft drop shadows for elevation. Typography should be a modern sans-serif (Inter) with dark slate text `#111827`. Inputs should be lightly bordered with a `#F9FAFB` fill. Buttons must be prominent and full-width where appropriate.
